# PinShot – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2026

**App:** PinShot – Smart Screenshot Organizer  
**Package:** com.alkisstam.pinshot

## Summary

**PinShot does not collect personal data, and the developer runs no servers.**  
Screenshots are processed on your device. If you turn on the optional "Your own API" feature, the text of a screenshot (or the image, when it has no text) and the searches you submit are sent to the AI provider you choose, using your own API key.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

**We collect NO data.** PinShot does not:

- Collect or transmit any personal information to the developer
- Connect to external servers, except the AI provider you set up yourself (Section 4), model downloads you start (Section 5) and Google ML Kit's model updates and usage metrics (Section 3)
- Store any data in the cloud
- Track your usage or behaviour
- Display advertisements

---

## 2. Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Read screenshots from your gallery for OCR analysis |
| `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Save restored screenshots to Pictures/PinShot when you restore a backup (Android 9 and older) |
| `READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED` | Read only the photos you pick, if you grant partial photo access (Android 14+) |
| `INTERNET` | Reach the AI provider you set up (Section 4) and download local AI models (Section 5) |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Resume watching for new screenshots after the phone restarts (can be disabled in Settings) |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Show new-screenshot notifications and reminders for dates found in screenshots (Android 13+) |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` / `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC` | Keep a full-library scan, library re-analysis or AI model download running with a progress notification when you leave the app |

The in-app camera and voice search open your phone's own Camera and speech apps, so PinShot itself needs no camera or microphone permission.

All permissions are used **only on your device** and for the stated purposes only.

---

## 3. Screenshot & OCR Processing

- Screenshots you add to PinShot are analyzed on your device using **Google ML Kit** (text recognition and barcode scanning) and **Tesseract** (text recognition)
- Categories, titles and details can also be generated by an on-device AI model (Gemini Nano or a local model you download). This runs entirely on your device
- Google ML Kit may contact Google servers to receive model updates and to send anonymous performance and usage metrics about the ML Kit APIs. This does not include your screenshots or their text. See Google's ML Kit terms for details
- Extracted text, categories, and metadata are stored **locally in a database on your device**
- Screenshot images and extracted text stay on your device, unless you turn on "Your own API" (Section 4)

---

## 4. Optional AI Providers

- In Settings → AI, you can enter your own API key for Google Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible service
- This feature is off by default. When it is on, PinShot sends the screenshot's text, or the image if it has no text, to that provider to get a category, title and details such as dates or addresses. It is used only when the on-device AI gives no answer
- When you submit a search (with the keyboard's search key or by voice), the words you searched for are sent to the same provider to turn them into search filters, again only when the on-device AI gives no answer. The search itself runs on your device, and no screenshots are sent with the query
- The request goes straight from your phone to the provider over an encrypted connection. The developer never receives it
- The provider handles that data under its own privacy policy and terms, for example Google's Gemini API terms or OpenAI's API data usage policy
- Your API key is stored only on your device and is not included in backups

---

## 5. Model Downloads

- If you choose a local AI model, PinShot downloads it from **Hugging Face**
- Only the standard download request is sent, which like any download includes your device's IP address. No screenshots or other personal data are included

---

## 6. Backup & Export (Cloud Sync)

- PinShot offers an optional backup feature that exports your data as a JSON file (text and details only) or, if you choose, a ZIP file that also contains your screenshot images
- This export is triggered manually by you and shared via the **Android system share sheet**
- You choose where to send the file (Google Drive, email, etc.) — PinShot does not upload anything automatically
- We have no access to any exported files

---

## 7. Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling PinShot removes all app data. Data sent to an AI provider you set up is kept according to that provider's policy.
